The backdrop is read from the cache only (ct_card_backdrop); a miss shows the poster, cropped. The check posts the same fields as the track modal's "Add watch" to episode_save, with the end date taken in the browser at the moment of the click (date only when TRACK_TIME is off), so history keeps the exact time. Without a next episode (caught up, or nothing aired yet) the card drops the check and shows the release year, as the poster card does. Context: item, media, next_episode (from ct_next_episode, may be None), user, TRACK_TIME, IMG_NONE. In Progress also passes remaining_seconds (time left, shown when known) and is_rewatch (the Rewatch badge). With a next episode the card opens that episode, otherwise the show.
